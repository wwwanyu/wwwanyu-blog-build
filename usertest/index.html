Participant Recruitment – Home Security User Study

Hi! My name is Wanyu, I'm a senior UI/UX designer conducting a user research study for a home security project. I'm doing this research to improve my design and am looking for participants who meet the following criteria:

Who we're looking for:

  1. Comfortable using English: the research involves using a mobile app with an English interface.
  2. Someone who has wanted to monitor something (e.g., home, pet, deliveries, vehicles etc.)
  3. Participants will receive NT$4,000 upon completion of the study as compensation

What's involved:

  • I will provide one recorder and three cameras for testing
  • I'll visit your home or business to observe and record the setup process
  • You'll keep the devices for 5 to 8 days and try using both the hardware and the mobile app
  • After the trial, we'll schedule a user test session to gather your feedback
  • That will conclude the study. At the end of the study, I’ll collect the devices. (Apologies — I’m conducting this research on my own resources 🥹)

Important:

  • The findings from this study may be included in my design portfolio, but no personal information or identifying details about participants will be shared.
  • The footage will be recorded on a MicroSD card inside the cameras. No cloud storage will be involved to ensure your privacy.
